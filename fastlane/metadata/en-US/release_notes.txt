Version 1.0 introduces Hawthorn as a local cocktail matcher for a small home bar. Stow bundled bottles, tag three into a tin, and shake to match a cocktail from a fixed set of recipes. Includes Discover match lines, Favorites with Pin and pours by day, Replay introduction, Contact Hawthorn, and Reset all data. Everything about the bar, tags, pins, and shake history stays on this device.
